## W13.T2

$I,H,D,R$ have rank 2, zero kernel, full image and determinant 1. $P,N$ have rank 1 and determinant 0. For $P$, kernel is span$(e_2)$ and image span$(e_1)$. For $N$, both kernel and image are span$(e_1)$. The columns directly give the basis-vector images.

## W13.T3

$I$: eigenvalue 1, whole plane, diagonalizable. $H$: eigenvalue 1, eigenspace span$(e_1)$, not diagonalizable. $D$: eigenvalues 2 and $1/2$, coordinate axes, diagonalizable. $P$: eigenvalues 1 and 0, coordinate axes, diagonalizable. $R$: no real eigenvalues, not diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$ (over $\mathbb C$ it has distinct eigenvalues $i,-i$). $N$: eigenvalue 0, eigenspace span$(e_1)$, not diagonalizable. Only $I,R$ preserve every length among these six cards.

## W13.T4

Valid repairs include: determinant 1 **and orthogonality** characterize orientation-preserving orthogonal maps in 2D; singular diagonal matrices have LU with $L=I$; a zero row in an $n\times n$ echelon matrix forces a free variable, but a rectangular tall full-column-rank matrix may have zero rows; diagonalizability requires enough independent eigenvectors; row reduction preserves rank and kernel, while a column-space basis must use pivot columns of the original matrix.

## W13.T5

Assess definition accuracy, fit of example, validity of counterexample, and a concrete verification method such as substitution, a nonzero kernel witness, dimension count, or checking a hypothesis. Do not grade the student’s personal confidence or disclosure of private AI conversations.

## Facilitation

After the 30-minute test: 20 minutes display construction, 20 minutes gallery discussion, 12 minutes claim repair, 8 minutes individual exit. Each team studies two cards; completing every card is optional revision.

Ask “which assumption makes that step valid?” before revealing a correction. In the final debrief, connect at least one calculation to the definition or theorem it illustrates. Use the exit response to choose next week’s short recap, not to rank students publicly.

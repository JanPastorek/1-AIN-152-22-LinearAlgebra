# Seminar 13 — The matrix museum: synthesis and explanation

[Course index](../README.md) · [Practice solutions](../solutions/practice/13_matrix_museum.md)

**Prerequisites:** All core weeks; eigenvalues over a specified field.

**Goal:** Connect algebraic, geometric and dynamical descriptions and defend conclusions.

**90-minute route:** 0–30 test/rehearsal; 30–50 T2; 50–70 T3; 70–82 T4; 82–90 exit.

**Working rules:** Commit to an individual prediction first. Work in pairs or groups of three, rotating explainer, skeptic and recorder. AI is allowed only in the investigation portions when the instructor permits it; record one claim you independently checked. Quizzes, tests and individual exits are tool-free unless an accommodation is agreed. No paid account is required. Optional tasks replace, rather than extend, the main activity.

## W13.T1

**First 30 minutes:** supervised test 2 if adopted, or a public rehearsal. The remaining hour is synthesis, not a new topic.

## W13.T2

Each team takes two cards from $I$, $H=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, $D=\operatorname{diag}(2,1/2)$, $P=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$, $R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. Make a display giving action on basis vectors, rank, determinant, kernel and image. Include one geometric sketch.

## W13.T3

For your two cards, find the real eigenvalues/eigenspaces, decide diagonalizability over $\mathbb R$, and decide whether every length is preserved. Each conclusion needs a reason. Exchange displays with another team and challenge one implication that is not valid.

## W13.T4

Choose one false statement and repair it with a hypothesis or counterexample: “determinant 1 means rotation”; “singular means no LU”; “a zero row means a free variable”; “two equal eigenvalues imply diagonalizability”; “row reduction preserves the column space.” Explain why the repaired version is true.

## W13.T5

**Individual exit and reflection:** Pick the concept you initially misunderstood most. State its definition, give an example and a counterexample to a tempting false claim. Explain one thing you can now verify in an AI-produced solution without asking another AI.

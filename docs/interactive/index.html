How to use a chapter page

Read, play, then check yourself
1 · DefinitionsRead the definition card, then watch its animation. Use the speed buttons to slow it down.
2 · LabsEach lab starts on an example from the book. Change the numbers, drag the vectors, and follow the “Try this” prompts.
3 · ExercisesExercises are taken from the book's exercise sets. Answers are checked exactly; fractions like −5/2 are accepted. Use Hint before Solution.
Notation, examples and exercise numbers follow Steven J. Leon, Linear Algebra with Applications, 8th edition. Animations were made with Manim Community; their source is in extras/manim/ of the course repository.

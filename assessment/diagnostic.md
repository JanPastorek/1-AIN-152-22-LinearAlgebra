# Short conceptual pre/post diagnostic

[Key](../solutions/practice/diagnostic.md) · [Teaching guide](../docs/teaching_guide.md)

Use form A near the start and B near the end, **10 minutes, ungraded**. “I have not learned this yet” is a valid pre-course response. Schedule outside the assessed 90-minute route or replace an activity; do not add it silently. These are original local items, not a validated concept inventory, and the forms are not psychometrically equated. Report item-level changes descriptively, without claiming the redesign caused them.

## Form A

1. Can three vectors span a plane without being independent? Give an example.
2. A $2\times3$ real matrix has rank 2. What is its nullity? Does the kernel contain one vector?
3. If $Ax=b$ has one solution $x_0$ and $Av=0$ with $v\ne0$, what other solutions follow?
4. Does determinant 1 imply preservation of lengths? Give a reason.
5. Is the zero vector an eigenvector? Explain.

## Form B

1. Can four vectors span a three-dimensional space without being independent? Give an example.
2. A $3\times4$ real matrix has rank 3. What is its nullity? Does the kernel contain one vector?
3. If $Cx=d$ has one solution $z$ and $Cw=0$ with $w\ne0$, what other solutions follow?
4. Does determinant −1 imply preservation of lengths? Give a reason.
5. If $M0=5\cdot0$, does that prove 5 is an eigenvalue? Explain.

# Final written and oral practice

[Assessment guide](README.md) · [Discussion guide](../solutions/practice/final_practice.md)

This is a **public revision bank**, not a specification of the official final exam or its internal weights. For peer rehearsal, draw one card, prepare for 3 minutes, explain for 3 minutes, and take one changed-assumption question. Give feedback on definitions, reasoning, and the hypothesis used; do not grade fluency or accent.

1. **Systems.** Prove that a nonempty solution set of $Ax=b$ is $x_0+\ker A$. Changed assumption: what if $b$ is not in the image?
2. **Groups.** Prove that invertible $n\times n$ real matrices form a group. Explain why all square matrices do not form a group with identity $I$. Changed assumption: multiplication of rectangular matrices of varying sizes.
3. **Rank–nullity.** State and prove the theorem using an extended kernel basis. Changed assumption: would rank 2 and nullity 3 be possible for a map with four-dimensional domain?
4. **Determinant.** Explain area/volume and orientation, and give a counterexample to “determinant 1 implies orthogonal.” Changed assumption: determinant zero.
5. **Projection.** Derive projection onto a nonzero vector and prove minimal distance. Changed assumption: a zero direction or a nonsymmetric idempotent matrix.
6. **Basis.** Derive $S^{-1}AS$ and explain why the order matters. Changed assumption: different bases in domain and codomain.
7. **Affine maps.** Show that translations preserve affine combinations but are generally not linear. Changed assumption: weights do not sum to one.
8. **Eigenvectors.** Explain diagonalizability and give a repeated-eigenvalue matrix that is not diagonalizable. Changed assumption: a real quarter-turn considered over $\mathbb C$.
9. **Application.** Given tomography's kernel direction $(1,-1,-1,1)$, characterize all extra sensor rows that remove ambiguity. Changed assumption: noisy, inconsistent measurements.
10. **Dynamics.** Analyze all two-state stochastic matrices $P(a,b)$ for $a,b\in[0,1]$. Changed assumption: $a+b=0$ or 2.

For written practice, choose cards 1, 3, 5 and 6 and write complete arguments before checking the guide. For calculation practice, use the two test pairs; avoid memorizing only one numerical example.

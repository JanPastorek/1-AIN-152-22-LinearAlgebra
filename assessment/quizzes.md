# Public practice quizzes — A/B forms

[Assessment guide](README.md) · [Keys](../solutions/practice/quizzes.md)

Each form: **8 minutes, 3 points**, one point for each numbered part. No AI or calculator is needed. A correct number without a requested explanation earns at most half of that part. These are public practice, not secure live forms.

## Quiz 1 — week 2: Matrix meaning

### Form A

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$: (1) compute $A(2,-1)^T$; (2) state $Ae_2$ and explain the column rule; (3) can $x+y=1$ and $2x+2y=3$ both hold? Give a certificate.

### Form B

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$: (1) compute $A(1,2)^T$; (2) state $Ae_1$ and explain the column rule; (3) can $x-y=2$ and $3x-3y=5$ both hold? Give a certificate.

## Quiz 2 — week 3: Exceptional parameter cases

### Form A

For $x+y=2$, $2x+ay=b$: classify and justify (1) $a=2,b=4$; (2) $a=2,b=5$; (3) $a=3,b=4$. Give a family or solution where appropriate.

### Form B

For $x+y=3$, $2x+ay=b$: classify and justify (1) $a=2,b=6$; (2) $a=2,b=7$; (3) $a=3,b=6$. Give a family or solution where appropriate.

## Quiz 3 — week 5: Inverses and LU

### Form A

(1) State $(AB)^{-1}$ for invertible square $A,B$ and verify one product. (2) Give unit-lower LU for $\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$; what does this show? (3) Under $PA=LU$, what are the two triangular systems for $Ax=b$?

### Form B

(1) State $(BA)^{-1}$ for invertible square $A,B$ and verify one product. (2) Give unit-lower LU for $\operatorname{diag}(0,2)$; what does this show? (3) Under $QA=LU$, what are the two triangular systems for $Ax=c$?

## Quiz 4 — week 7: Spaces and dimensions

### Form A

A $3\times4$ matrix has rank 2. (1) State its nullity and kernel ambient space. (2) How many vectors are in a column-space basis, and in what ambient space? (3) Does the kernel contain exactly two vectors? Explain.

### Form B

A $4\times5$ matrix has rank 3. (1) State its nullity and kernel ambient space. (2) How many vectors are in a column-space basis, and in what ambient space? (3) Does the kernel contain exactly two vectors? Explain.

## Quiz 5 — week 9: Determinant interpretation

### Form A

(1) Compute $\det\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$. (2) State area scale and orientation change. (3) Give a determinant-1 matrix that does not preserve every length, with a witness vector.

### Form B

(1) Compute $\det\begin{pmatrix}-3&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$. (2) State area scale and orientation change. (3) Give a determinant-1 matrix that does not preserve every length, with a witness vector.

## Quiz 6 — week 12: Eigenvector definition

### Form A

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$: (1) check whether $e_1$ is an eigenvector and give its eigenvalue; (2) check $v=(1,-1)^T$; (3) is the zero vector an eigenvector? State the relevant definition.

### Form B

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$: (1) check whether $e_2$ is an eigenvector and give its eigenvalue; (2) check $v=(1,-1)^T$; (3) is the zero vector an eigenvector? State the relevant definition.

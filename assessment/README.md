# Assessment proposal — requires lecturer approval

[Course index](../README.md) · [AI rules](ai_policy.md) · [Semester schedule](../docs/semester_plan.md)

This package does **not** change official grading. It proposes a way to distribute the existing semester component. Confirm the current course information sheet, local calendar, accommodations, and lecturer agreement before announcing a live scheme.

The inspected course information sheet specifies semester/final weighting **50/50**, at least **55% of semester points** to proceed, and a final examination with written and oral parts, including at least **50% on the written part**. It does not specify the internal written/oral split here; this package does not invent one. With a semester component normalized to 50, the semester gate is 27.5/50. The final-exam gate remains separate.

| Proposed semester component | Timing | Maximum | Evidence of individual learning |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Two supervised tests | Weeks 6 and 13; 30 minutes each | 20 | 10 points each, individual |
| Six short quizzes; best five count | Weeks 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12; 8 minutes each | 15 | 3 points each, individual |
| Two application labs | Weeks 5 and 12, inside the seminar | 10 | Each: 1 group artifact + 4 individual transfer points |
| Five practice/correction submissions | After weeks 2, 4, 7, 10, 12 | 5 | One point each; AI allowed with verification |
| **Total** | | **50** | **43 points from supervised individual work** |

Tests replace the first 30 minutes of those seminars. Quizzes replace the opening activity. Lab checks replace the exit question. There is no additional weekly oral examination. The final oral part remains governed by the lecturer's rules. The five homework tasks target about 20–30 minutes each; collect time estimates during the pilot and reduce scope if needed.

## Lab marking, each out of 5

- **Group artifact, 1 point:** a coherent model with a prediction, a checked computation or plot/table, and a mathematical explanation. A screenshot alone does not earn the point. Group size 2–3; allow an equivalent individual submission where needed.
- **Individual transfer, 4 points:** one point per specified step in the week 5 or week 12 solution. Partial credit is based on independent reasoning, not on whether the group used AI.

An alternative method earns the same credit if valid. An early arithmetic slip should not remove later method credit when the subsequent reasoning is consistent. Do not award points for the sophistication or cost of tools.

## Practice bank and security

Everything in this repository is public. The A/B forms below are **practice and calibration material**, not secure live tests. Renaming numbers is insufficient security if the task is otherwise identical. Prepare live variants privately, check their mathematics and timing, and use a brief unfamiliar transfer step. Never commit live keys here, including to an “instructor” folder in this public repository.

The A/B forms target similar content and timing; equivalent difficulty has **not** been empirically established. Use sample student work to calibrate, and moderate marking across seminar groups. Best-five quizzes allow one missed/low attempt. Agree a published, equitable make-up route for further documented absences and accommodations; do not invent penalties or exceptions case by case. Retakes and final grade conversion must follow the course's actual regulations.
